# Customer Behavior Analysis Dashboard 📊📊

A dynamic Power BI dashboard engineered to track, analyze, and uncover deep insights into customer purchasing patterns, subscription trends, and product preferences. This project translates raw transactional data into actionable business strategies to optimize customer retention and maximize revenue.

## 💡 Key Business Insights Discovered
Customer Base: The dataset analyzes a total of 3.9K Customers with an average purchase value of $59.76.

Subscription Split: Non-subscribers make up the vast majority of the customer base at 73%, while active subscribers make up 27%.

Demographic Trends: Young Adults are the highest revenue-generating age group, followed closely by Middle-aged customers. Interestingly, while Seniors generate slightly less total revenue, they beat out regular Adults in total Sales volume (order frequency).

Product Performance: Clothing is the absolute powerhouse of this business, generating the highest overall Revenue and highest Sales volume across all categories, followed by Accessories.

## 🛠️ Features & Functionality
* **Cross-Filtering & Interactions:** Interactive visuals allow deep-dives into specific categories (e.g., clicking on a category filters the entire customer demographic profile seamlessly).
* **Dynamic Slicers:** Tile-based category slicers optimized for intuitive, user-friendly navigation.
* **KPI Formatting:** Custom color-coded visual cards highlighting essential metrics like `% of Customers by Subscription Status`, `Revenue by Category`, and `Sales Volume`.
